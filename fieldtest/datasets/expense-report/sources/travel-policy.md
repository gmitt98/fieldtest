# Meridian Corp — Travel & Expense Policy (rev. 2026-03)

## 4.1 Daily limits

- **Meals** are reimbursed up to **$75 per day**. Amounts above the cap are the
  employee's responsibility; the rest of the receipt is still reimbursed.
- **Lodging** is reimbursed up to **$250 per night**, excluding taxes and fees.
  Amounts above the cap are the employee's responsibility.
- **Ground transport** (taxi, rideshare, rail, parking) has no daily cap.

## 4.2 Airfare

Economy fare only. Premium economy, business, and first class require written
approval from a VP before booking.

## 4.3 Exclusions

The following are **never reimbursable**, regardless of amount or approval:

- Alcohol
- In-room entertainment
- Fines, penalties, and traffic citations
- Personal items purchased during travel

## 4.4 Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for any expense over **$25**. Expenses at or
below $25 may be claimed without one.
