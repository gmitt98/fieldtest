# Clearbook Support — Refund Policy (rev. 2026-05)

## 2.1 Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge is refunded in full, with no time limit. Verify the
duplicate against the charge record before issuing anything.

## 2.2 Standard refunds

Refundable within **30 days** of the order date. Outside 30 days, the agent
must **escalate** to a human reviewer rather than refund.

## 2.3 Limits

- An agent may refund up to **$200** without approval. Above $200, escalate.
- Refund exactly the amount charged. Never round, and never estimate.

## 2.4 Before refunding

Always read the order and its charges first. An agent that issues a refund
without reading the charge record has not verified that the charge exists.
